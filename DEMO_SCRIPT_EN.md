## Recording rules
- Capture actual app interactions. Label any retained receipt screenshot as a prior verified test, with its date.
- Do not show passwords, keys, developer secret panels or personal login pages.
- Never splice in a mocked success receipt or call the token a goods purchase.
- Preserve the completed Sandbox session. Do not create another payment for filming.
- If source data or a criterion is wrong, do not narrate it as a successful exact match.
- No unlicensed music or decorative third-party material. Review the complete video before public upload.
- Public YouTube upload and final submission remain separate actions.
